Features

Everything the dispenser can do

From precise stepper rotation to real-time logging — explore the capabilities that make Mediminder reliable.

Scheduling

7-Rack Sequential Dispensing

Configure up to 7 medication racks with date- and time-specific schedules. The system automatically advances to the next pending rack after each dispense.

Date + Time Auto-advance Pending / Dispensed
Hardware

Precise Stepper Rotation

Each rack rotates by a configurable degree (default 52°) to align the correct compartment with the dispensing tray — driven by an ESP32-controlled stepper motor.

ESP32 Stepper Motor 1°–360°
Timekeeping

Real-Time Clock Sync (PHT)

The server broadcasts Philippine Standard Time (UTC+8) to the ESP32’s RTC, ensuring every dose is dispensed at the exact scheduled moment — even after power loss.

PHT / UTC+8 RTC Sync ISO 8601
Monitoring

Dispense & Intake Logging

Every dispense event and patient intake (drawer-open via sonar) is timestamped and logged — giving caregivers a full audit trail of medication adherence.

Event Logs Sonar Intake Audit Trail
Safety

Low-Rack Warnings & PWA

Get alerted when remaining racks fall below a configurable threshold, and install Mediminder as a Progressive Web App for offline-capable access from any device.

Threshold Alerts Offline Support Installable
Why Mediminder

Built for real adherence challenges

Medication non-adherence is a leading cause of treatment failure, especially among elderly patients and those with complex regimens. Mediminder addresses this head-on.

Forgotten Doses

Up to 50% of patients miss scheduled doses. Automated, time-stamped dispensing eliminates forgetfulness as a failure mode.

Caregiver Visibility

Remote caregivers can verify each dose was dispensed and retrieved — no guessing, no phone calls, just a live log.

Refill Awareness

Low-rack warnings notify caregivers before the dispenser runs empty, so refills happen on time, every time.

Adherence Tracking

A complete history of dispense and intake events makes it easy to spot patterns and share data with healthcare providers.

Hardware-Software Sync

The ESP32 RTC stays synced to Philippine Standard Time, so the physical dispenser and the web dashboard always agree.

Accessible Anywhere

As an installable PWA with offline support, Mediminder works on any phone or desktop — no app store required.
